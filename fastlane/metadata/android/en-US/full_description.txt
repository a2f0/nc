Noise Connoisseur plays white noise and pink noise, generated live on your device. Two sounds, done well.

The noise is made in real time, not looped from a recording, so there's no repeating pattern or seam to notice. Use it to sleep, focus, study, or cover up a noisy room.

• White noise: equal energy at every frequency. Bright and crisp.
• Pink noise: softer highs, deeper lows. Like steady rain.
• Keeps playing in the background and with your phone locked. Stop it from the lock screen or the notification.
• Home screen widget: start and stop each sound with one tap.
• Its own volume slider, so the noise can sit quieter than your other audio. It's remembered between launches.
• Fades gently in and out.

No ads, no accounts, no subscription, and no tracking. The app doesn't use the internet, and it collects no data.
