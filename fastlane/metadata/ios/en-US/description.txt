Noise Connoisseur plays white, pink, and brown noise, a fan, and ocean waves, all generated live on your device. Five sounds, done well.

Every sound is made in real time, not looped from a recording, so there's no repeating pattern or seam to notice. Use it to sleep, focus, study, or cover up a noisy room.

• White noise: equal energy at every frequency. Bright and crisp.
• Pink noise: softer highs, deeper lows. Like steady rain.
• Brown noise: mostly lows, muffled highs. A deep, soft rumble.
• Ocean waves: surf rolling in and washing out, never the same twice.
• Fan: a steady stream of air over a low hum.
• Keeps playing in the background and with your phone locked. Stop it from the lock screen or Control Center.
• Home screen widget: start and stop each sound with one tap.
• Its own volume slider, so the noise can sit quieter than your other audio. It's remembered between launches.
• Fades gently in and out.

No ads, no accounts, no subscription, and no tracking. The app doesn't connect to the internet, and it collects no data.
