Support

Questions, problems, or ideas for Noise Connoisseur? Email support@a2f0.net. Mention your device and its iOS or Android version if something isn't working.

Does it keep playing in the background?

Yes. Noise keeps playing when you switch apps or lock your phone, and you can stop it from the lock screen, Control Center, or the notification shade.

Is there a widget?

Yes. Add the Noise Connoisseur widget to your home screen to start and stop each sound with one tap.

How does the volume slider work?

The slider in the app sets the noise level within your device's volume, so you can keep the noise quieter than other sounds. It's remembered between launches.

Refunds

Apple and Google handle purchases. To request a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem or Google Play's refund page.
