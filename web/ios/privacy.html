Privacy Policy for iPhone and iPad

Effective October 23, 2026 (UTC)

Noise Connoisseur for iPhone and iPad collects no data. We don't receive your settings, listening history, or playback log. There are no accounts or ads, and the app contains no analytics, tracking, or third-party code that collects data.

On your iPhone or iPad

The noise is generated on your device as you listen, and the app doesn't connect to the internet. Links you open from it, such as Support, Privacy Policy, or a review on the App Store, open in Safari or the App Store app. The app remembers a few settings on your device: your volume, appearance, language, and which sound is playing or was last played (so the home screen widget can show it). It also saves listening history on your device: the sound played, its start and stop times, and checkpoints every 15 seconds during playback. This history is used to show your listening chart.

The home screen widget and the Play Noise and Stop Noise actions in Shortcuts work through the app on your device and don't send anything anywhere.

These settings and listening history stay on your device and are never sent to us. iCloud Backup and backups to a computer may include them, under Apple's terms. To erase them, delete the app.

Playback log

A local playback log keeps the latest 300 events to help explain unexpected stops: timestamps, sounds, playback commands, audio interruptions, audio route and engine changes, and termination notices from iOS. Each event's details are limited to 512 characters. The app never uploads this log. You can view it in More → Settings → Playback log and choose to share it; the share sheet sends the text only to the app or person you select. The log is excluded from device backups and is removed when you delete the app.

The App Store

Apple handles downloads and payments under its privacy policy; we never see your payment details. Apple gives developers sales and download reports and, if you've turned on Share With App Developers in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, crash reports. We use these only to understand and fix problems with the app.
