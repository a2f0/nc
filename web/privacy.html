Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Noise Connoisseur is a white and pink noise player for iPhone, iPad, Android, and the web at nc.a2f0.net. It doesn't collect, store, or share personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking, and the apps contain no third-party code that collects data.

On your device

The noise is generated on your device as you listen. The iPhone, iPad, and Android apps don't connect to the internet. The web version downloads only its own files from nc.a2f0.net and never sends your settings or what you listen to. The apps remember a few settings on your device, such as your volume and which sound is playing (so the home screen widget can show it).

These settings stay on your device and are never sent to us. Your device's own backups may include them, under Apple's or Google's backup terms. To erase them, delete the app, or on the web, clear this site's data in your browser.

The website

nc.a2f0.net is hosted by Cloudflare. As with any website, Cloudflare receives the technical details your browser sends to load a page, such as your IP address and browser type, and uses them to deliver the page and protect the site, as described in Cloudflare's privacy policy. We don't add cookies, analytics, or tracking to the site.

App stores

Apple and Google handle downloads and payments under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google); we never see your payment details. They give developers sales and download reports and, if you've chosen to share diagnostics with developers in your device settings, crash reports. We use these only to understand and fix problems with the app.

Children

Noise Connoisseur is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@a2f0.net.
